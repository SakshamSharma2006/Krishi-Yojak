## 📌 Overview  
**Krishi Yojak** is a smart multilingual farming app designed to empower small and marginal farmers with **simple, timely, and accessible crop guidance**.  
The app bridges the gap between generations of farmers by combining **traditional wisdom** with **modern agricultural practices**.  

---

## 🚀 Features  
- 🌾 **Crop Advisory** – Right advice at the right time for soil, pesticides, medicines, and farming practices.  
- 🗣️ **Multilingual Support** – Hindi, Marathi, English, Punjabi.  
- 📱 **Simple UI** – Easy-to-use design for even old-age farmers.  
- 📊 **Government Schemes & Services** – Easy access to farming schemes, subsidies, and resources.  
- 💧 **Irrigation Reminders** – Timely alerts to save water and improve yields.  
- 🌱 **Fertilizer Recommendations** – Personalized suggestions for soil health.
